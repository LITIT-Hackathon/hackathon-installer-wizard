# Definition of Ready

A story is ready when:

- Persona, job, evidence/finding, and intended outcome are named.
- Priority and MVP/stretch classification are agreed.
- Acceptance criteria are observable and testable, including failure/recovery.
- Content and accessibility behavior are specified.
- Platform dependencies, mock/real boundary, data contract, and assumptions are explicit.
- Design/component reuse is identified; required states exist in the inventory.
- Open questions have owners or are marked future validation requirements.
- The story is small enough for one focused implementation slice.
